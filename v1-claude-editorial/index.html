How much does a website cost in Calgary?

Most Calgary web designers quote somewhere between about $2,000 and $7,000 for a small business website, and many don't publish prices at all. Ours are on this page: Launch is $3,900 one-time for a custom site of up to 6 pages. Grow is $1,200 a month plus $1,500 setup, and adds local SEO, Google Business Profile management and 60-second lead follow-up. Scale is $2,500 a month plus your ad spend. All prices are in CAD, plus GST.

Do you do local SEO and Google Business Profile management in Calgary?

Yes. On Grow and Scale we manage your Google Business Profile (categories, photos, posts and review requests), build service pages for what you sell, and track where you rank on Google Maps in Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Chestermere and Okotoks. You see the results on your live dashboard.

What's in the free audit?

A short written report covering your page speed, mobile experience, Google Business Profile, where you rank on the map for your main searches, and the five changes most likely to bring in more calls. It's yours to keep, whether or not you work with us.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Monthly plans are month-to-month. If we're not earning our fee, you can leave, and you keep your website, domain and data.

How long does a new website take?

About four weeks from the plan call to launch for most sites. You'll get a fixed launch date before we start.

Which platform do you build on?

It depends on what you need. We use fast, agency-grade builders for most sites, Wix or Squarespace if you want to edit it yourself, and Shopify for online stores.

What is AI search optimization?

More customers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI answers for a recommendation. We structure your site, profile and reviews so these tools understand your business and suggest it.

How do I know it's working?

We set up tracking before we change anything, so we have a true starting point. Your live dashboard then shows calls, form fills, bookings and rankings against that starting point every month.
