FAQ

Good questions.

Still wondering about something? Call (403) 555-0142 or email hello@opengrowth.ca.

How much does a website cost in Calgary?

Most Calgary web designers quote somewhere between about $2,000 and $7,000 for a small business website, and many don't publish prices at all. Ours are on this page: Launch is $3,900 one-time for a custom site of up to 6 pages. Grow is $1,200 a month plus $1,500 setup, and adds local SEO, Google Business Profile management and 60-second lead follow-up. Scale is $2,500 a month plus your ad spend. All prices are in CAD, plus GST.

Do you do local SEO and Google Business Profile management in Calgary?

Yes. On Grow and Scale we manage your Google Business Profile (categories, photos, posts and review requests), build service pages for what you sell, and track where you rank on Google Maps in Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Chestermere and Okotoks. You see the results on your live dashboard.

What's in the free audit?

A short written report (usually 2 to 4 pages) covering your website's speed, mobile experience, calls-to-action and tracking, plus your Google Business Profile: categories, photos, reviews and how you rank on the map for your main searches. Each issue comes with a plain-English fix, ranked by impact. You get it within 3 business days.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

It's free and there's no obligation. The audit shows you how we think. Plenty of owners fix things themselves using the report, and that's fine. If you'd like help, we'll suggest a plan with a published price.

How long does a new website take?

Most Launch sites go live in about 3 to 5 weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly we can get your photos, services and details. We'll give you a firm timeline in your quote.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Grow and Scale are month-to-month, so you can stop whenever you like. Launch is a one-time project, and the care plan is monthly and optional.

Who owns the website if I leave?

You do. Your site, domain, Google profile, analytics and lead data are yours. If you leave, we hand over logins and files, no fees, no hostage situations.

What is AI search optimization?

More people ask tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews for recommendations. We structure your site's content, business details and schema markup so those tools can clearly understand what you do and where, which makes you more likely to be mentioned.

How does the 60-second lead follow-up work?

When someone fills in your form or you miss a call, an n8n automation sends them a friendly text and email straight away with next steps, alerts you with their details, and logs the lead to your dashboard. The messages are written with you and sound like you.

Do you only work with certain industries?

No. We work with local businesses that rely on calls, bookings and quote requests. If people find you on Google and then contact you, we can help.

Why don't you show client reviews yet?

Because we're new and won't invent them. Our founding client program exists to earn real, tracked results and real Google reviews. Those will appear here as they come in.
