FAQ

Straight answers.

Can't find yours? Call (403) 555-0142 or email hello@opengrowth.ca.

What's in the free audit?

We look at your website (speed, mobile experience, how easy it is to call or book) and your Google Business Profile (categories, photos, reviews and where you rank on Google Maps for your main searches). You get a short written report within 3 business days with the top fixes in plain English. It's yours to keep, whether or not you hire us.

How much does a website cost in Calgary?

Most Calgary web designers quote somewhere between about $2,000 and $7,000 for a small business website, and many don't publish prices at all. Ours are on this page: Launch is $3,900 one-time for a custom site of up to 6 pages. Grow is $1,200 a month plus $1,500 setup, and adds local SEO, Google Business Profile management and 60-second lead follow-up. Scale is $2,500 a month plus your ad spend. All prices are in CAD, plus GST. We publish them because you shouldn't need a sales call to find out if you can afford something.

Do you do local SEO and Google Business Profile management in Calgary?

Yes. On Grow and Scale we manage your Google Business Profile (categories, photos, posts and review requests), build service pages for what you sell, and track where you rank on Google Maps in Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Chestermere and Okotoks. You see the results on your live dashboard.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Monthly plans are month-to-month, so you can cancel whenever you like. We'd rather keep you because the numbers are going up.

Who owns the website if I leave?

You do. The site, the domain, your Google profile and all your data are in your name. If you leave, we help you move everything over.

What is the founding client program?

Open Growth is new, so we're offering five businesses a reduced founding rate. In return, we track your before-and-after numbers (calls, form fills, bookings, map ranking) and publish them as a case study, and if you're happy, you leave an honest Google review. Tick the box on the audit form to apply.

Do I need to learn Power BI to use the dashboard?

No. You open a link and see your numbers: calls, leads, bookings, reviews and rankings. We build it, connect it and keep it updated. You just read it.

What does the 60-second follow-up actually do?

When someone fills in your form, requests a booking or calls while you're busy, an automation built in n8n sends them a friendly text reply within a minute and sends you their details right away. The message is written in your voice and you approve it before it goes live.

What is AI search optimization?

More customers now ask tools like ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI answers for recommendations. We structure your site's content and business details so those tools can understand what you do, where you work and why you're a good choice.

Do you work with businesses in my industry?

Most likely. We work with local service businesses that want more calls, bookings or enquiries. The free audit is the easiest way to find out if we're a good fit.

What platform do you build on?

It depends on you. If you want us to handle everything, we use a fast agency platform. If you'd like to edit the site yourself, we can build on Wix or Squarespace. Online stores go on Shopify. We'll recommend one in your audit.
